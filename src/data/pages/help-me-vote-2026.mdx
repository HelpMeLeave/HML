The midterm elections is our best chance to turn things around in United States and give more time for those seeking options to leave, so we've put together this election guide that has helpful steps to **getting registered to vote**, **how to plan your vote**, and **what your state's key deadlines are**.

Whether you are someone looking to leave the U.S. or wanting to stand up for the rights of at-risk Americans, this guide is for you. **Let's turn out the vote together**.
